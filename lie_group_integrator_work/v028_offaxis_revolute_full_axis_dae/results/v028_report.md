# v028 Experiment Report

Generated by `run_v028.py`.

## Purpose

- Stress-test v027 with an off-axis body torque that a revolute joint should balance through reaction torques.
- The new `*_fullva` methods keep the square residual but additionally replace the x/z orientation and angular-velocity collocation equations with hinge-axis velocity and acceleration constraints.
- Keep raw Gauss4/Gauss6, v026 `*_pivotvc`, and v027 `*_pivotva` as baselines.
- Keep Brown-McPhee friction dependent on the stage reaction-force norm so JAX differentiates the coupled friction/multiplier residual.
- Use fine-step Gauss6 FullVA as the reference because this version tests full-axis consistency rather than raw Gauss6 drift.

## Interpretation

- FullVA is a stricter revolute-joint consistency test than v027 PivotVA because it also enforces hinge-axis velocity and acceleration components inside the stage residual.
- The off-axis torque gives the joint multipliers something nontrivial to balance in the constrained axes, which reduces the risk that zero axis diagnostics are only a symmetry artifact.
- In this revolute pendulum, FullVA tightens axis velocity/acceleration diagnostics from about 1e-12 to 1e-25 but does not change the trajectory relative to PivotVA; the main practical improvement remains the pivot velocity/acceleration repair from v027.
- This remains a revolute-specific square residual, not a general lower-pair/TFE weighted-residual formulation.
