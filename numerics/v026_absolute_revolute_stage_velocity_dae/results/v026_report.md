# v026 Experiment Report

Generated by `run_v026.py`.

## Purpose

- Test a square stage-level velocity-consistent residual for v023's absolute-coordinate quaternion revolute DAE.
- The `*_pivotvc` methods replace stage `r` collocation with the pivot velocity constraint while retaining pivot position and hinge-axis position constraints.
- Keep raw Gauss4/Gauss6 and the projected Gauss6 repair as baselines.
- Keep Brown-McPhee friction dependent on the stage reaction-force norm so JAX differentiates the coupled friction/multiplier residual.
- Endpoint and stage pivot/axis velocity constraint parts are reported separately.

## Interpretation

- Stage pivot velocity constraints substantially reduce endpoint velocity drift without step-after projection.
- PivotVC is square and differentiable through the same JAX friction/multiplier residual, unlike the overdetermined full pos+vel+acc stage consistency residual.
- PivotVC improves robustness: in the sharp case, Gauss4 succeeds at the two smaller step sizes where raw Gauss4 fails.
- It is still partial index-3 consistency: hinge-axis velocity is trivial in this one-DOF test, and full lower-pair velocity/acceleration treatment remains open.
- Projected Gauss6 remains the strict endpoint-closure baseline; PivotVC is a less intrusive in-residual repair.
