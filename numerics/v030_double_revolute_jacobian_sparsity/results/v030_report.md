## Interpretation

- Smooth Gauss6 FullVA has dimension 138 and density 0.031; sharp Gauss6 FullVA density is 0.031.
- The best sparse/dense solve ratio observed is 0.00x for `double_revolute_smooth full_va G6`.
- The worst sparse/dense solve ratio observed is 3.21x for `double_revolute_smooth raw G4`.
- For Gauss6 specifically, sparse/dense solve ratios range from 4.932e-04x to 3.095e-03x; CSR `spsolve` is decisively faster for the 138-dimensional systems in this environment.
- For Gauss4, the 92-dimensional systems are too small for generic CSR to be consistently better; sparse overhead sometimes loses.
- The immediate v031 engineering target should be integrating sparse/block linear solves into the Gauss6 Newton loop. The deeper target is to avoid materializing a dense AD Jacobian by exploiting block/stage sparsity or using structured Newton-Krylov.
