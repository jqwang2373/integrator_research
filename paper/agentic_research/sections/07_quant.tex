\paragraph{Effort}
Table~\ref{tab:artefacts} summarizes the artefacts. The transcript of the final session (the
manuscript rewrite) is profiled in Figure~\ref{fig:session}: about a third of its tool calls were
validators and manuscript builds, a third inspection and editing of files, a tenth Lean, and a
twentieth experiments. Earlier phases were not recorded as transcripts and are documented by the
version ledger and the dated logs.

\paragraph{What the human said}
The transcript has \nEntries{} user-role entries, but \nHarness{} of them are generated by the
harness and \nShell{} are shell commands; \nHuman{} were typed by the human. They fall into six kinds
(Appendix~\ref{app:messages}): goals (eleven), approvals of the agent's own proposals (nine),
authorizations (three), one decision, judgments (three) and questions (six); the rest were
acknowledgments. None contained a technical instruction about the method, the proofs or the
experiments. The three judgments produced Incidents 1 and 4 of Table~\ref{tab:incidents} and the
layout overhaul of the manuscript; the one decision set the horizon of every chain experiment, and
went against the agent's recommendation.

\paragraph{What the agent did without being asked}
The agent chose the experiment set and its reference policy (Richardson floor, fit only above
$100\times$ floor), found and fixed the orientation-angle formula, discovered the regular-branch
limit and escalated it, added the model-alignment check for the public comparison, wrote the
solver-envelope experiment that falsified its own claim, froze the old ledger, and wrote the memory
files for the next session. It also produced the three human-caught errors.

\paragraph{Cost of the checks}
The frozen ledger's two validation chains take about twenty minutes, the builder sequence that
refreshes their snapshots ten, the new manuscript validator two seconds, and the Lean check under a
minute once the Mathlib cache is present. The longest single computation was the $h=10^{-4}$
double-pendulum reference over $T=3$ (1.6~h), computed once and cached.
