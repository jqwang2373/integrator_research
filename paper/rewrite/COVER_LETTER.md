# Cover letter

Dear Editors of Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering,

Please consider the manuscript "A sixth-order Lie-group Gauss collocation integrator for lower-pair
mechanisms with position-, velocity- and acceleration-level constraints" for publication as a
research article.

The paper studies a three-stage Gauss collocation step for constrained multibody systems in absolute
coordinates whose stage system enforces the joint constraints at position, velocity and acceleration
level together with the Newton–Euler balance, and reconstructs the rotational endpoint through the
exponential map. Its central result is an exact algebraic identity: the implemented 132-row stage
system has the lifted reduced Gauss stage as its unique local root, so the method is Gauss
collocation of the reduced joint-coordinate equations of motion executed in absolute coordinates.
From the identity we obtain a sixth-order error bound whose only standing hypothesis is smoothness
on a compact neighbourhood of the trajectory; the solver and inverse interfaces are derived for small
steps and the small-step threshold is quantified and traced to the curvature of the friction law.
The algebra, the perturbation chain and the Butcher conditions are machine-checked in Lean 4 with
Mathlib. Numerically we show sixth order on a frictional two-body chain and on the ASME double
pendulum, roundoff-level reproduction of the three driven ASME mechanisms including the closed loops,
the work/precision advantage over the lower-order members of the same Gauss family, a comparison
with the public absolute-coordinate codes against one common reference, and the order reduction
induced by a sharp friction law.

All numbers and figures are generated by scripts from result files in the accompanying repository,
which also contains the Lean development. The manuscript has not been published and is not under
consideration elsewhere. There are no competing interests.
