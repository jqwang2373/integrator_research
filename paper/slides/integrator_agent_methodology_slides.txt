Endpoint closure is its own sandbox




      Why it matters. A projection can make constraints look clean while changing the one-
      step map. The agent cannot hide that inside an order claim.

Caveats are first-class evidence




             Friction smoothness boundary.                           Sparse correctness versus sparse speed.



   Agent behavior. Do not delete caveats. Promote smooth-order evidence, and demote
   sharp-friction and runtime caveats to their correct claim lanes.

How to build this agent for our integrator work




      1      encode MethodSpec       2      pin ProblemSpec              3   choose sandbox level


      4      run numerical ladder    5      update proof ledger          6   gate paper claims



      Good autonomy here means fewer unverified claims, not fewer human research
                                     decisions.
